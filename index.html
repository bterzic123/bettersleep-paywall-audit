What this is

We went through BetterSleep's onboarding as a new user, up to your paywall, and checked it against our new report.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and State of Paywalls 2026 is what we learned from 78,000+ of their paywalls. We installed BetterSleep, answered your sleep quiz, reached the paywall, tried to leave it, and read your App Store listing. Then we checked each part of the paywall against the report.

There are four ideas, and each one is built into a clickable prototype of your paywall. The prototype uses your design, not ours. Benchmarks are for Health & Fitness apps in the US, your App Store category and your biggest storefront.

We did not redesign your app

The paywall in the prototype is yours: your black canvas, the cloud mascot, the blue highlight in the headline, the white “Try for free” pill and your own “Transaction canceled” sheet. We only added things you already have, like your awards, your rating and your trial reminder.

You may have tested some of this

We can only see the paywall from outside, so we don't know what you have already tested. If you have tried any of these, tell us and we'll spend the time on the others.
